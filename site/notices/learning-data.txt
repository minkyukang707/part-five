Online sentences: Tatoeba contributors, selected/packaged by ManyThings.org.
https://www.manythings.org/anki/  https://tatoeba.org/
Each imported sentence retains its Tatoeba sentence ID and attribution in feedback.
Only English originals are used from non-Korean packs. Re-used originals are transformed
into fill-in-the-blank exercises; grammar explanations and answer options are added locally.
Originals carry CC BY 2.0 FR as indicated on each source line.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

Academic and news imports: the app selects one sentence per eligible article per download,
replaces one expression with a gap, and supplies choices and Korean grammar notes.
Europe PMC: only research abstracts marked "cc by" are accepted. Feedback gives
the credited authors, work title, license, and the specific article with its license details.
https://europepmc.org/RestfulWebService  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
NASA: English editorial text, with article title, source URL and feed byline when present.
https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/feed/  https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
Images and logos are not imported. Classroom examples labeled by subject are original exercises.
