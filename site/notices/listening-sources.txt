Changes for this application
Four excerpts from the opening verse of each recording are selected.
Capitalization and punctuation are normalized for dictation. Korean
learning hints and answer comparison are original additions by Part Five.
The audio and video are played by the official YouTube embedded player.
The source media are not extracted, re-hosted or offered for download.
The artist does not sponsor or endorse Part Five.

License evidence and scope
These particular official descriptions say Creative Commons Attribution
without naming a version. Part Five retains that wording and links to
the specific creator statements rather than assigning a version.
Attribution identifies the title, artist, source and modifications.
The selection relies on the original songwriter's licensing statement
for his own performance and lyrics.
This is a checked permission basis, not a guarantee of legal clearance
for every country, future licensing change or later third-party upload.

User-created listening exercises
The optional YouTube input loads only the official embedded player.
Users supply their own transcript or lyrics that they are authorized to use.
The application does not retrieve YouTube audio or subtitles.
User-provided URLs, excerpts and attempts are stored in the user's browser.
Opening the embedded player contacts YouTube under YouTube's policies.
