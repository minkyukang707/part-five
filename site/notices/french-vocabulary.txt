Part Five · French vocabulary · 2026-11-20

Content: 2,000 distinct French headwords and expressions, with Korean learner
glosses. 67 sets (66 × 30 words, final set 20), and five in-app difficulty
levels of 400 words each. Levels are a pedagogical ordering based on core
vocabulary and corpus frequency, not an official CEFR certification.

3. Editorial adaptation
   The dictionary subset is normalized, filtered, shortened into quiz answers,
   deduplicated (including œ/oe aliases), and manually reviewed in a 2,000-word
   list. Approximately 495 entries use explicitly edited learner glosses.
   Examples of corrected raw-source mistakes: coûter (비용이 들다), toit (지붕),
   compagnie (동행·회사), prêter (빌려주다), stade (경기장), feu (불).
   Primary reference checks for disputed meanings:
   https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/article/A9C4693 (coûter)
   https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/article/A9C3188 (compagnie)
   https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/article/A9P4232 (prêter)
   https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/article/A8T0713 (toit)
   These reference definitions are not copied. The Korean learner wording is
   an editorial adaptation, not a complete account of every sense of a word.

   Noun gender is only attached to noun senses. Missing or ambiguous gender is
   left blank rather than guessed; grammatical gender is not inferred from a
   player's gender. un/une are learning cues, and les vacances is plural.
   IPA is included only where supplied by a dictionary. être and vraiment use
   the headword transcription checked at https://fr.wiktionary.org/wiki/être
   and https://fr.wiktionary.org/wiki/vraiment. Other transcriptions may retain
   regional/alternative pronunciation notation from the source. No invented
   Korean phonetic transcription is included. Browser speech uses fr-FR.
   Synonyms/antonyms are not inferred from co-occurrence or ambiguous links.

   The field reviewed records dictionary comparison/editorial screening; it
   does not claim professional native-speaker proofreading of every sense.
   Derived vocabulary data and these adaptations are distributed under
   CC BY-SA 4.0. This attribution applies to the vocabulary data, independently
   of the application's code license.
