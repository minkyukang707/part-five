Changes: selected one spelling-compatible reading for each vocabulary entry,
retained authored starter readings, and added contextual reading overrides.
Multiple pronunciations can exist; one common reading is shown for study.
Derived reading data in japanese-readings.js is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Korean pronunciation is a programmatic approximate reading aid, not IPA and not
a representation of pitch accent. Kana guidance and lesson examples are authored
for this app. Curriculum references are linked in the grammar study screen.

Maintenance: obtain the current official JMdict_e.gz and run
python -X utf8 scripts/build-japanese-readings.py PATH/TO/JMdict_e.gz
when refreshing vocabulary/readings. Review contextual homographs before release.
