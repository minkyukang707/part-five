Foreign-language expansion, 2026-11-18

Sentence sources
Tatoeba contributors, distributed by ManyThings Anki bilingual exports.
https://www.manythings.org/anki/
https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
CC BY 2.0 FR, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Every imported exercise retains the contributing usernames and sentence IDs in its explanation.
Changes: theme classification, expression selection, blanking, answer choices, Korean study notes.
These are original-expression reconstruction exercises. Other expressions can be grammatically valid.
The English sentence is the supplied meaning aid. Korean grammar notes are application-authored.

Vocabulary sources
Extended Open Multilingual Wordnet, Wiktionary-derived Korean, Japanese, Spanish and Turkish lemma tables.
https://omwn.org/
https://github.com/omwn/omw-data
https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data/blob/gh-pages/packages/corpora/extended_omw.zip
Original table headers specify CC BY-SA and Wiktionary, http://wiktionary.org/.
The imported vocabulary alignment remains available under the source CC BY-SA terms.
Changes: shared WordNet synset alignment to Korean, deduplication, ranking by corpus occurrence,
selection of 2,000 distinct entries per language including the original 60 entries, and 30-entry sets.
Synset alignment is automatic and can contain rare senses or imperfect Korean equivalents.
Selected common meanings are editorially corrected in scripts/language-meaning-overrides.json.
Entries and sentence classifications have structural validation and sample review, not exhaustive native-speaker review.

Rebuild
scripts/build-language-expansion.py takes a directory with extended.zip, jpn.zip, spa.zip and tur.zip,
and reads the original courses from languages-data.js using Node.js (an optional second argument supplies a JSON snapshot).
Requires Python 3 and Node.js 24, with no third-party packages. The original 60 word IDs and 48 exercise IDs are retained.
