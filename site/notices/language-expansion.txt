Sentences
Tatoeba contributors, distributed by ManyThings Anki bilingual exports.
https://www.manythings.org/anki/
https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
CC BY 2.0 FR, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Every imported exercise retains contributor usernames and sentence IDs in its explanation.
Changes: theme classification, expression selection, blanking, answer choices, Korean notes and meaning hints.
These are original-expression reconstruction exercises; other answers can be grammatically possible.

Korean meaning hints
The English reference meaning is retained as sourceMeaning for traceability, not shown as the question hint.
6,516 imported exercises use 6,263 distinct saved Korean translations, with additional editorial corrections.
Translations were prepared locally using Helsinki-NLP / OPUS-MT eng-kor:
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-ko
Original Marian release: opusTCv20210807-sepvoc_transformer-big_2022-07-28, CC BY 4.0.
https://github.com/Helsinki-NLP/OPUS-MT-train
Tiedemann and Thottingal (2020), OPUS-MT – Building open translation services for the World.
No GPT, translation API, or model download is used by learners. Only the resulting text is shipped.
Checks include coverage, broken text, negative clauses, selected idioms and sample review.
These checks do not amount to exhaustive professional/native-speaker review; errors can remain.

Revised vocabulary
Korean Wiktionary contributors, structured extraction by Wiktextract / Kaikki:
https://ko.wiktionary.org/
https://kaikki.org/kowiktionary/rawdata.html
https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract
Source dump: 2026-11-01, extraction: 2026-11-02. Wiktionary content is under its CC BY-SA terms.
Per-entry Korean Wiktionary source links are included where used.
Changes: entry matching, short-gloss selection, removal of metadata/unsuitable senses, frequency ranking,
editorial corrections, and grouping into 30-entry sets. Derived glosses remain under the source share-alike terms.
The original 6,000 entries were compared against direct Korean entries; unsupported automatic alignments
were replaced with sourced vocabulary. Original authored beginner entries and explicit corrections are retained.
The review counts, changed meanings, removed entries and replacements are in vocabulary-review.json.
There are still exactly 2,000 entries per language. Retired attempts are archived rather than transferred to new words.

Rebuild
scripts/build-language-expansion.py uses local extended.zip, jpn.zip, spa.zip and tur.zip plus Node.js 24.
When reviewed data exists, it runs scripts/apply-language-review.cjs, which applies the saved
language-ko-hints.json, language-hint-overrides.json and language-reviewed-words.json.
New source sentences without a Korean translation fail validation rather than silently showing an English hint.
The default site build does not translate or recollect material. Translation models are not in this repository.
